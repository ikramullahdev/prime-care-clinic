Privacy Policy

Information on patient data privacy, website demonstration status, and confidentiality safeguards.

🔒

Demonstration & Concept Notice

This website is currently an independent architectural demonstration and design concept. All interactive appointment forms operate in demo mode and do not record, store, or transmit your personal or health data over the internet.

1. Commitment to Patient Privacy

Prime Care Clinic is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our patients, visitors, and users of our digital platforms. Medical information is among the most sensitive personal data, and we adhere strictly to established principles of medical confidentiality and ethical data stewardship.

2. Information Collected in Live Production

When a secure backend appointment service is officially configured and authorized by clinic management, the following categories of information may be collected strictly for clinical and scheduling purposes:

  • Contact details: Patient full name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Scheduling preferences: Desired consultation category (General Physician, Diabetes Care), requested appointment date, and preferred time window.
  • Patient notes: Brief optional message or chief symptoms provided voluntarily by the patient to assist the doctor with preparation.

3. Use of Personal Information

Personal data gathered through official appointment channels is utilized strictly to:

  • Schedule, verify, confirm, or reschedule clinical appointments.
  • Send appointment reminder notifications or schedule updates.
  • Maintain internal clinical administrative records in accordance with healthcare standards.

We do never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose patient personal information to third-party marketing or commercial organizations under any circumstances.

4. Data Security and Architecture

Our appointment management system architecture employs industry-standard security safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic is routed over secure HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Database Protection: Appointment records in PostgreSQL utilize parameterized queries and ORM abstractions to prevent injection attacks.
  • Role-Based Access: Only authenticated, credentialed clinic personnel have access to administrative dashboards and patient rosters.
  • Token-Based Authentication: Administrative operations require cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with restricted lifetimes.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies, cross-site behavioral advertising pixels, or invasive surveillance scripts. Session storage is limited to necessary technical application state.

6. Patient Rights

Patients retain the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of their contact information from scheduling records by contacting the clinic management directly.

7. Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy is reviewed periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
