Before Your Visit

Planning ahead helps ensure a smooth and productive visit. Here are some key things to prepare.

📋
Bring your medical records Previous test results, prescriptions, and referral letters if applicable
💊
List your current medications Include dosage, frequency, and any supplements you take
📝
Note your symptoms When they started, how often they occur, and what makes them better or worse
❓
Prepare your questions Write down anything you want to ask so nothing is missed during your consultation
