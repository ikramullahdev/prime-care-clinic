General Physician Consultations

Thorough medical assessments for a wide range of health concerns.

Primary Care

Your Health, Carefully Assessed

A general physician consultation is the starting point for most healthcare needs. Whether you are experiencing new symptoms, managing an existing condition, or seeking preventive health advice, our general consultations provide a thorough evaluation of your health.

The physician will review your medical history, discuss your current concerns, perform a clinical examination, and recommend appropriate diagnostic tests or treatments based on the findings.

Scope of Care

What We Can Help With

Our general consultations address a variety of common health concerns and conditions.

Your Visit

What to Expect During Your Consultation

Before Your Visit

  • Gather any previous medical records, recent lab results, or imaging reports.
  • Make a list of all current medications, including dosages and frequencies.
  • Write down your symptoms: when they started, how often they occur, and any factors that affect them.
  • Prepare a list of questions you would like to discuss with the physician.

During the Consultation

The physician will begin by discussing your medical history and current concerns. A clinical examination will be performed as appropriate. Based on the assessment, the physician may recommend diagnostic tests, prescribe medications, suggest lifestyle modifications, or refer you to a specialist if needed.

You are encouraged to ask questions, discuss concerns, and ensure you understand the recommended plan before leaving the consultation.

After Your Visit

Follow the prescribed treatment plan and keep track of your symptoms. If prescribed medications, take them as directed and note any side effects. Attend scheduled follow-up appointments for ongoing conditions. Contact the clinic if your symptoms worsen or if you have concerns about your treatment.
