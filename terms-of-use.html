This website is not designed for emergency response. In the event of a medical emergency—including acute chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden slurred speech or numbness, severe trauma, or acute diabetic ketoacidosis—immediately call Rescue 1122 or proceed directly to an emergency department (such as CMH Rawalpindi or District Headquarters Hospital).

Completing an appointment request form on this website constitutes a request for scheduling, not a guaranteed or confirmed booking. In demo mode, no appointment is created. In live production with a connected backend, appointments remain in "pending" status until clinical staff verify doctor availability and issue formal confirmation.
