Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our clinic and services.

Appointments & Visits

While walk-in visits may be accommodated when possible, we strongly recommend requesting an appointment in advance. This helps minimize your waiting time and ensures the physician can dedicate adequate time to your consultation. You can request an appointment through our appointment page.
You can submit an appointment request through our website using the appointment request form. Please note that the online form is currently in demonstration mode. You may also contact the clinic directly to schedule. The clinic will confirm your appointment time after reviewing your request.
Please bring: (1) A valid form of identification such as your CNIC, (2) Any previous medical records, lab results, or diagnostic reports, (3) A list of all current medications with dosages, (4) A written summary of your current symptoms and concerns. See our Patient Information page for a complete preparation guide.
Reported operating hours are: Monday – Thursday: 5:30 PM – 10:00 PM, Friday: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM, Saturday: 5:30 PM – 10:00 PM, Sunday: Closed. Please note that these hours are sourced from directory listings and may vary. We recommend confirming with the clinic before your visit.
Yes. If you need to reschedule or cancel, please contact the clinic as early as possible. This allows the appointment slot to be offered to another patient and helps the clinic manage its schedule effectively.

Services & Care

Our known services include general physician consultations and diabetes care and management. General consultations cover a wide range of health concerns including routine check-ups, chronic condition management, and preventive health guidance. Diabetes care includes blood sugar monitoring guidance, medication management, dietary counseling, and complication screening.
Yes. Diabetes care is one of our primary areas of focus. We offer support for patients with Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, including blood glucose monitoring guidance, medication management, lifestyle and dietary counseling, complication screening, and ongoing follow-up care. Learn more on our Diabetes Care page.
This is an outpatient clinic, not an emergency facility. If you are experiencing a medical emergency — such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or dangerously low blood sugar — please call emergency services or go directly to the nearest hospital emergency department.
Yes. If the physician determines that your condition requires specialist evaluation, they can provide guidance on appropriate next steps and referrals to relevant specialists or facilities in Rawalpindi or nearby areas.
